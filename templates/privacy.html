Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 1, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect only the information necessary to provide the service:

  • Account information: your name and email address provided at registration.
  • Expense data: amounts, categories, dates, and notes you enter into the app.
  • Usage data: basic logs such as login timestamps, used solely for security and debugging.

We do not collect payment information, government identification, or any sensitive financial account details.

2. How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used exclusively to:

  • Provide and maintain your personal expense tracking dashboard.
  • Authenticate your account and keep it secure.
  • Respond to support requests or account issues you raise with us.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond delivering the service to you.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured servers. We take reasonable technical measures to protect it against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. However, no method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. We may use limited third-party services (such as hosting providers) solely to operate the platform. These providers are bound by confidentiality obligations and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact information provided on our website. We will respond as promptly as possible.
