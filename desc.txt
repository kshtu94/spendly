                    Models in Claude Code.

. Opus -- most powerful but most expensive . Used for
complex programming tasks.


. Sonnet -- the default for most users . Best balance of speed,
quality and token cost. Good for everyday coding tasks.


. Haiku -- fastest and cheapest . Use for simple , repetitive or
exploratory tasks where you don't need deep reasoning.



##################################################################
                        VV.IMp Concept

A common power-user pattern is to use "Opus" for the planning phase
-- where you're thinking through architecture , writing specs , making
decisions -- then switch to "Sonnent" for the implementation phase where
the thinking is done and you just need reliable code generation.
